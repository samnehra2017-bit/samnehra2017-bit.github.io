OpenAll Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

OpenAll (package com.openall.viewer) is a file viewer for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. In short: your files never leave your device. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising and billing services need to show ads and process purchases.

1. Your files

OpenAll opens files that you choose through the Android file picker, "Open with" or "Share". The app does not request general storage access. Files are read, displayed, edited and saved entirely on your device. OpenAll does not upload, copy to a server, scan or analyse your files, and we (the developer) never see them.

Some features create temporary copies in the app's private cache, for example when extracting a file from an archive or preparing a document for display. Android deletes this cache when needed, and you can clear it any time in Settings → Apps → OpenAll → Storage.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Recent files: the names and locations of files you opened recently, so you can reopen them. You can remove single entries or clear the list from the home screen.
  • Playback positions: where you stopped in audio and video files, so playback can resume.
  • Purchase status: whether you have bought "Remove ads", so ads stay hidden.

This data is not sent to us. Android's own backup feature may include it in your Google account backup if you have backup turned on; that is controlled by your device settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of OpenAll shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID (or app set ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and diagnostic information such as crash data from the SDK.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, OpenAll asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later from the app menu ("Privacy settings"). Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices). Buying "Remove ads" stops ads from being requested.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

"Remove ads" is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; OpenAll never sees your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which product you bought, so it can unlock it. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers or collect analytics.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or collect your name, email, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.

6. Children

OpenAll is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall OpenAll. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept under Google's retention policies; you can manage it at My Ad Center and by resetting your advertising ID.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
